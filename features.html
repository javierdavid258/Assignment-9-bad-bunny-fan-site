Who He's Worked With

Explore Bad Bunny's music collaborations over the years.

Collaboration Timeline

  1. 2017

    First Big Collaboration - "Majores" w/ Becky G

    Featured on Becky G's debut solo album, "Mala Santa." This song received a lot of recognition and won many awards, including a Latin American Music Award for Favorite Urban Song in 2018.

  2. 2018

    "I Like It" w/ J Balvin and Cardi B

    This track became a massive hit, reaching the top of the charts in multiple countries and establishing Bad Bunny as a major force in the music industry.

  3. 2019

    "Oasis" w/ J Balvin

    This collaborative album with J Balvin showcased Bad Bunny's versatility and ability to blend different musical styles. It debuted in the top 10 of the Billboard 200 chart.

  4. 2020

    "Safarea" w/ Ñengo Flow & Jowell y Randy

    One of the collaborations on his album "YHLQMDLG", this song received critical acclaim for its innovative sound and achieved strong commercial success, becoming a cultural phenomenon and an instant hit.

  5. 2021

    "Mia" w/ Drake

    This collaboration with Drake blended their unique styles and featured lyrics in both English and Spanish. It brought Bad Bunny further into the spotlight in the English-speaking music market.

  6. 2022

    "Ojitos Lindos" w/ Bomba Estéreo

    One of the many collaborations off of his critically acclaimed album, "Un Verano Sin Ti". This grammy-winning track fuses Colombian folk music with reggaeton.

  7. 2023

    "Perro Negro" w/ Feid

    Collaborating with Feid, Columbian singer, this track peaked at #1 in Colombia and #4 on the Billboard Global 200 chart.

  8. 2025

    "CAFe CON RON" w/ Los Pleneros de Cresta

    A standout from his 2026 Super Bowl halftime show, this song blends traditional merengue with reggaeton, celebrating Puerto Rican identity.
